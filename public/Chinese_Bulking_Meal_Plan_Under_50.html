🔪 MONDAY BULKING BATCH COOKING GUIDE

  1. Step 1: Prep & Weigh the Chicken Breast: Cut all ~5.0 lbs of raw chicken breasts into bite-sized 1-inch cubes for wok searing.
  2. Step 2: Wok Sear & Glaze: Toss chicken cubes with 1 tsp cornstarch and garlic powder. Sear in 0-cal canola spray for 6-8 minutes until golden brown and 165°F internal. Toss with your General Tso's Glaze (Panda Express sauce, soy sauce, sweetener packet, ginger, garlic powder, water). Divide across 14 glass meal prep containers (7 lunches, 7 dinners).
  3. Step 3: Cook DOUBLE Bulk White Rice: Rinse 7 cups of dry white rice (from your massive 5 lb bag!). Cook with 14 cups of water until tender and fluffy. Divide 2 full cups of cooked rice into each of your 14 containers alongside the General Tso's chicken for maximum bulking glycogen!
  4. Step 4: Steam Frozen Broccoli: Portion 1 cup of frozen broccoli florets into each container next to the chicken and double rice.
  5. Step 5: Daily 5-Egg Breakfast & Oats: Every morning at 10:30 AM after your gym session, scramble 5 whole eggs in 0-cal canola spray with scallions and soy sauce, and prepare 1 cup of warm cinnamon oatmeal for high-density, hormone-boosting muscle building nutrition!
