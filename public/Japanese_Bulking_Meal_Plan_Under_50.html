🔪 COMPLETE STEP-BY-STEP PREPARATION, COOKING, STORAGE & PORTION GUIDE

Follow this exact protocol on Monday (Batch Prep Day) and daily throughout your bulking week so every single gram bought on your Auburn Walmart grocery run is prepared perfectly and accounted for:

  1. Step 1: Exact Quantities & Portioning from Your Walmart Grocery List:
    • Fresh Chicken Breast (~5.0 lb family tray / 80 oz total raw): Divide into 14 equal raw portions of ~5.7 oz (~160g) each. Once seared, each raw 5.7 oz cube shrinks to ~4.0 oz cooked meat. You consume exactly two 4.0 oz cooked portions per day (Lunch & Dinner) = 8.0 oz cooked daily total (73g protein).
    • Plain Nonfat Greek Yogurt (Three 32 oz Tubs = 96 oz weekly total): Portion out exactly 14 oz (~1.75 cups) daily across two servings: 8 oz (1 cup) at 5:30 PM and 6 oz (3/4 cup) at 10:30 PM.
    • Large Grade A White Eggs (36 Count Tray / Two 18 ct Cartons): Use exactly 5 whole Grade A eggs daily for your post-workout Tamagoyaki scramble (30 eggs across 6 lifting days, plus 6 extra eggs for Sunday active recovery or restock). Natural cholesterol optimizes anabolic hormones during bulking!
    • Great Value Old Fashioned Rolled Oats (42 oz Canister): Use exactly 1/2 cup dry oats (~40g) cooked with water daily (yields 1 full cooked cup) alongside your post-workout 5-egg scramble.
    • Long Grain White Rice (5 lb Bulk Bag): Cook 7 cups dry rice right after shopping on Monday to yield 14 cups cooked. You eat exactly 4 cooked cups (~632g cooked total) daily divided across Lunch (2 cups DOUBLE PORTION) and Dinner (2 cups DOUBLE PORTION). Plus cook 1 cup for post-workout recovery.
    • Frozen Broccoli Florets (Two 12 oz Bags = 24 oz total): Portion exactly 2 cups (~170g) daily across your Lunch and Dinner containers (1 cup each).
  2. Step 2: Wok Searing & Caramelizing the Japanese Teriyaki Chicken (Monday Batch Cook):
    Open your ~5.0 lb family tray of chicken breasts. On a clean cutting board, slice all chicken into bite-sized 1-inch cubes. Place all cubes in a large mixing bowl and toss lightly with 2 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tbsp garlic powder, and 1 tsp black pepper. Coat your large non-stick wok or skillet with Great Value 0-Calorie Canola Oil Cooking Spray over medium-high heat. Add chicken cubes in two batches to avoid crowding. Sear for 6-8 minutes until golden brown on all sides and internal temperature reaches 165°F.
    The Low-Calorie Teriyaki Glaze: In a measuring cup, whisk together 10 tbsp Kikkoman Teriyaki Marinade, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 7 zero-calorie sweetener packets, 1 tsp ground ginger, 1 tsp garlic powder, and 1/4 cup warm water. Pour the glaze directly over the cooked chicken in the wok over medium heat. Toss vigorously for 1-2 minutes until the sauce thickens into a glossy, caramelized coating that clings to every cube!
  3. Step 3: Batch Cooking Fluffy White Rice (DOUBLE PORTION) & Portioning Broccoli:
    Rinse 7 cups of dry white rice in a fine mesh strainer until water runs clear (removes excess surface starch for fluffy grains). Combine with 14 cups of water in your large stovetop pot or rice cooker. Bring to a boil, cover tightly, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 18 minutes. Fluff gently with a fork.
    Container Assembly: Lay out 14 glass meal prep containers (7 for Lunch, 7 for Dinner). Spoon exactly 2 cups of cooked white rice (DOUBLE PORTION for bulking glycogen expansion) into each container. Next right next to the rice, spoon exactly one 4.0 oz cooked portion of your glazed Teriyaki Chicken. Finally, place 1 cup of frozen broccoli florets into each container directly beside the chicken.
  4. Step 4: Exact Storage & Reheating Protocol (No Moisture Loss or Spoilage):
    • Fridge vs. Freezer Storage: Seal all 14 glass containers tightly with leak-proof snap lids. Place Containers 1 through 8 (Tuesday through Friday meals) in the coldest part of your refrigerator (34°F–38°F) where they stay fresh for up to 4 days. Place Containers 9 through 14 (Saturday through Monday meals) directly into your freezer (-0°F). On Friday evening, transfer the frozen containers to the fridge to thaw safely overnight.
    • Precision Reheating: When ready to eat Lunch or Dinner, pop open the container lid and cover the food loosely with a damp paper towel. Microwave on High for exactly 2 minutes 30 seconds (3 minutes if slightly icy due to the double rice volume). The damp paper towel creates steam inside the glass container, perfectly steaming the broccoli florets while restoring the chicken teriyaki and double rice to fresh, juicy, just-cooked perfection!
  5. Step 5: Daily Morning Scramble & Supplementation Protocol:
    Every morning at 8:00 AM right before beginning your Quiet Apartment Bodyweight Dojo workout and 45m Cardio protocol, brew 2 tbsp of Death Wish Espresso Roast with 8 oz hot water and pour over ice alongside eating 1 fresh banana. Upon returning inside at 10:30 AM, spray a clean non-stick skillet with 0-cal canola oil spray over medium heat. Crack 5 whole Grade A eggs directly into the skillet. Gently scramble with a silicone spatula for 2-3 minutes. Right before taking off the heat, fold in 2 tbsp chopped fresh Japanese Negi (green onions) and 1 tbsp soy sauce until golden and fluffy. Serve right alongside your 1 cup of warm cinnamon rolled oats and 1 tsp Sriracha Chili Garlic!
    • Weekly Wild Pink Salmon Omega-3 Protocol: Once per week (recommended on Sunday or Wednesday active recovery days), open your 14.75 oz can of Wild Pink Salmon. Mix with 1 tsp soy sauce and scallions or serve over 1 cup of rice. This delivers potent EPA/DHA Omega-3 fatty acids essential for joint lubrication, reduced systemic inflammation from heavy bulking sets, and skin elasticity support (`160 lbs clean goal without loose skin`).
