🔪 MONDAY BULKING BATCH COOKING GUIDE & SALMON / COFFEE PROTOCOLS

  1. Step 1: Prep & Weigh the Chicken Breast: Open your ~5.0 lb family tray of chicken breasts from Auburn Walmart. Cut all raw chicken breasts into bite-sized cubes. Batch sear all cubes at once in your non-stick wok or skillet using 0-cal canola oil spray.
  2. Step 2: Wok Sear & Teriyaki Glaze: Toss chicken cubes lightly with 1 tsp cornstarch and garlic powder. Sear over medium-high heat for 6-8 minutes until golden brown and 165°F internal. Whisk low-cal Japanese Teriyaki Glaze (`1.5 tbsp Kikkoman Teriyaki marinade, 1 tsp soy sauce, 1 zero-cal sweetener packet, 1/4 tsp ginger, 1/4 tsp garlic powder, 1 tbsp water per daily portion`). Pour over cooked chicken and toss vigorously for 1 minute until sticky, glossy, and caramelized! Divide evenly across 14 glass containers (7 lunches, 7 dinners).
  3. Step 3: Cook Bulk White Rice (Bulking DOUBLE Portion): Cook 7 cups of dry white rice with 14 cups of water in a large pot or rice cooker until tender and fluffy. Divide 2 full cups of cooked rice into each of your 14 containers alongside the Chicken Teriyaki for maximum muscle glycogen expansion!
  4. Step 4: Steam Frozen Broccoli: Portion 1 cup of frozen broccoli florets into each container right next to the chicken and rice. The broccoli will naturally thaw inside the sealed container in the fridge and steam perfectly when microwaved for 2 minutes!
  5. Step 5: Morning 5-Egg Tamagoyaki & Pre-Workout Coffee Schedule: Every morning at 8:00 AM, brew 2 tbsp of Death Wish Espresso Roast over ice and eat 1 banana right before your Planet Fitness lifting & treadmill workout. At 10:30 AM right after training, scramble 5 whole Grade A eggs in 0-cal canola spray with chopped fresh Japanese Negi (scallions) and soy sauce alongside 1 cup of warm cinnamon rolled oats!
  6. Step 6: Weekly Wild Pink Salmon Omega-3 Protocol: Once per week (recommended on Sunday or Wednesday active recovery days), open your 14.75 oz can of Wild Pink Salmon. Mix with 1 tsp soy sauce and scallions or serve over 1 cup of rice. This delivers potent EPA/DHA Omega-3 fatty acids essential for joint lubrication, reduced systemic inflammation from heavy bulking sets, and skin elasticity support (`160 lbs clean goal without loose skin`).
