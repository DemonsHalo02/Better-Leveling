🔪 MONDAY BULKING BATCH COOKING & MEAL PREP GUIDE

  1. Step 1: Prep & Weigh the Chicken Breast: Open your ~5.0 lb family tray of chicken breasts from Auburn Walmart. Cut all raw chicken breasts into bite-sized cubes. Batch sear all cubes at once in your non-stick wok or skillet using 0-cal canola oil spray.
  2. Step 2: Wok Sear & Glaze: Toss chicken cubes lightly with 1 tsp cornstarch and garlic powder. Sear over medium-high heat for 6-8 minutes until golden brown and 165°F internal. Whisk low-cal Japanese Teriyaki Glaze (Kikkoman Teriyaki marinade, soy sauce, sweetener packet, ginger, garlic powder, water). Pour over cooked chicken and toss vigorously for 1 minute until sticky, glossy, and caramelized! Divide evenly across 14 glass containers (7 lunches, 7 dinners).
  3. Step 3: Cook Bulk White Rice (Bulking DOUBLE Portion): Cook 7 cups of dry white rice with 14 cups of water in a large pot or rice cooker until tender and fluffy. Divide 2 full cups of cooked rice into each of your 14 containers alongside the Chicken Teriyaki for maximum muscle glycogen expansion!
  4. Step 4: Steam Frozen Broccoli: Portion 1 cup of frozen broccoli florets into each container right next to the chicken and rice. The broccoli will naturally thaw inside the sealed container in the fridge and steam perfectly when microwaved for 2 minutes!
  5. Step 5: Refrigerate & Crush Your Volume: Store all 14 containers in the refrigerator. Every morning at 8:00 AM grab your Death Wish iced coffee and banana, make your fresh 5-egg Japanese Tamagoyaki scramble & rolled oats at 10:30 AM, and pop your double-portion Teriyaki bowls into the microwave at 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM!
