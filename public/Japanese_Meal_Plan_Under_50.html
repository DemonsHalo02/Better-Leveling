🔪 MONDAY BATCH COOKING GUIDE & SALMON / COFFEE PROTOCOLS

  1. Step 1: Prep & Weigh the Chicken Breast: Open your ~5.0 lb family tray of chicken breasts from Auburn Walmart. Cut all raw chicken breasts into bite-sized 1-inch cubes. Batch cook all cubes at once in your non-stick wok or skillet using 0-cal canola oil spray.
  2. Step 2: Wok Sear & Teriyaki Glaze: Toss chicken cubes lightly with 1 tsp cornstarch and garlic powder. Sear over medium-high heat for 6-8 minutes until golden brown and 165°F internal. Whisk together your low-calorie Japanese Teriyaki Glaze (`1.5 tbsp Kikkoman Teriyaki marinade, 1 tsp soy sauce, 1 zero-cal sweetener packet, 1/4 tsp ginger, 1/4 tsp garlic powder, 1 tbsp water per daily portion`). Pour over cooked chicken and toss vigorously for 1 minute until sticky, glossy, and caramelized! Divide evenly across 14 glass meal prep containers (7 lunches, 7 dinners).
  3. Step 3: Cook Bulk White Rice: Rinse 3.5 cups of dry white rice in a strainer until clear. Cook with 7 cups of water in a large pot or rice cooker until tender and fluffy. Divide 1 cup of cooked rice into each of your 14 containers alongside the Chicken Teriyaki.
  4. Step 4: Steam Frozen Broccoli: Portion 1 cup of frozen broccoli florets into each container right next to the chicken and rice. The broccoli will naturally thaw inside the sealed container in the fridge and steam perfectly when you pop the container into the microwave for 2 minutes!
  5. Step 5: Morning Scallion Tamagoyaki & Pre-Workout Coffee Schedule: Every morning at 8:00 AM, brew 2 tbsp of Death Wish Espresso Roast over ice and eat 1 banana right before your Planet Fitness lifting & treadmill workout. At 10:30 AM right after training, scramble 3 whole eggs + 4 oz liquid whites in 0-cal canola spray with chopped fresh Japanese Negi (scallions) and soy sauce for rapid muscle repair!
  6. Step 6: Weekly Wild Pink Salmon Omega-3 Protocol: Once per week (recommended on Sunday or Wednesday active recovery days), open your 14.75 oz can of Wild Pink Salmon. Mix with 1 tsp soy sauce and scallions or serve over 1 cup of rice. This delivers potent EPA/DHA Omega-3 fatty acids essential for joint lubrication, reduced systemic inflammation from lifting, and skin elasticity support (`160 lbs clean goal without loose skin`).
