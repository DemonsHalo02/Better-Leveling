📦 Master Step-by-Step Meal Prep & Batch Cooking Tutorial

👨‍🍳 Monday Batch Prep Directive (How to Prep, Cook, Portion & Store)
Total Time: ~45 Minutes

Follow this systematic Monday workflow right after your grocery run so that from Tuesday through the following Monday, all your meals take under 90 seconds to serve!

  • Step 1: Rice & Vegetable Batch Steam: Rinse 3.5 cups of dry Long Grain White Rice in cold water until clear. Cook in rice cooker or large pot with 7 cups water and a pinch of salt until fluffy (~20 minutes). Simultaneously, steam both 4 lb bags of frozen broccoli florets until crisp-tender (~6 minutes). Drain well so broccoli isn't watery.
  • Step 2: Healthy Crispy Fried Chicken & Teriyaki Glazing: Trim any excess fat from your ~5.0 lb family pack of chicken breasts and cut into uniform 1-inch bite-sized cubes. Dip cubes lightly in whisked egg white and toss in a large bowl with 1 tbsp garlic powder, 1 tsp salt, and cornstarch. Spray air-fryer basket (or non-stick skillet) generously with 0-cal canola spray. Air-fry in batches at 400°F (or crisp sear) for 10–12 minutes until super crispy and crunchy outside and cooked through (165°F internal). Whisk together your Japanese Teriyaki Glaze (8 tbsp Kikkoman Japanese BBQ Marinade, 4 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp sriracha, 4 zero-cal sweetener packets, 1 tsp ginger, and 1/4 cup water). Pour over the crispy cooked chicken and toss vigorously until sticky, glossy, and caramelized!
  • Step 3: Portioning Into Containers: Set out 14 glass or BPA-free meal prep containers (7 for Lunch, 7 for Dinner). In each container, place exactly 1 cup of cooked white rice, 1.5 cups of steamed broccoli, and ~5.7 oz of Pan or Oven Crispy Fried Japanese Teriyaki Chicken. Seal with airtight lids.
  • Step 4: Fridge & Freezer Storage Rules: Store Containers 1 through 6 (first 3 days of lunches and dinners) directly in your refrigerator. Place Containers 7 through 14 in the freezer to keep the chicken completely fresh and juicy. On Thursday evening, move the remaining containers from the freezer to the fridge to thaw overnight for the rest of your week!
