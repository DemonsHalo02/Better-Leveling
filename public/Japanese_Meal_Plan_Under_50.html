🔪 MONDAY BATCH COOKING & MEAL PREP GUIDE

  1. Step 1: Prep & Weigh the Chicken Breast: Open your ~5.0 lb family tray of chicken breasts from Auburn Walmart. Cut all raw chicken breasts into bite-sized 1-inch cubes. Weigh out daily 11.4 oz portions or batch cook all cubes at once in your non-stick wok or skillet using 0-cal canola oil spray.
  2. Step 2: Wok Sear & Glaze: Toss chicken cubes lightly with 1 tsp cornstarch and garlic powder. Sear over medium-high heat for 6-8 minutes until golden brown and 165°F internal. Whisk together your low-calorie Japanese Teriyaki Glaze (Kikkoman Teriyaki marinade, soy sauce, zero-cal sweetener packet, ginger, garlic powder, water). Pour over cooked chicken and toss vigorously for 1 minute until sticky, glossy, and caramelized! Divide evenly across 14 glass meal prep containers (7 lunches, 7 dinners).
  3. Step 3: Cook Bulk White Rice: Rinse 3.5 cups of dry white rice in a strainer until clear. Cook with 7 cups of water in a large pot or rice cooker until tender and fluffy. Divide 1 cup of cooked rice into each of your 14 containers alongside the Chicken Teriyaki.
  4. Step 4: Steam Frozen Broccoli: Portion 1 cup of frozen broccoli florets into each container right next to the chicken and rice. The broccoli will naturally thaw inside the sealed container in the fridge and steam perfectly when you pop the container into the microwave for 2 minutes!
  5. Step 5: Refrigerate & Enjoy Tuesday – Monday: Seal all 14 containers and store in the refrigerator. Every morning at 8:00 AM grab your Death Wish iced coffee and banana before your bodyweight workout, make your fresh 3-minute scallion & liquid egg white Tamagoyaki scramble at 10:30 AM, and pop your Wok-Seared Teriyaki bowls into the microwave at 2:00 PM and 7:30 PM!
