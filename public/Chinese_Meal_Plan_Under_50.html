📋 Daily 6-Meal Timeline & Full Cooking Instructions

8:00 AM (Pre-Workout) Pre-Workout Fuel: Death Wish Iced Black Coffee & Banana
110 kcal | 1g Protein | 27g Carbs | 0g Fat
  • Pre-Workout Black Coffee: Brew 2 tbsp Death Wish Espresso Roast ground coffee with hot water and pour over ice (or batch cold-brew overnight). Optional: stir in 1 zero-calorie sweetener packet for sweetness. High-octane clean caffeine before Planet Fitness Lewiston! (5 kcal, 0g p)
  • Pre-Gym Carbohydrates: 1 Medium Fresh Banana peeled and eaten right at 8:00 AM for rapid potassium and glycogen fuel (105 kcal, 1g p, 27g c)
10:30 AM – 11:00 AM (Post-Workout) Post-Workout Breakfast: Scallion Soy Egg Scramble + Rice & Tea
550 kcal | 34g Protein | 46g Carbs | 25g Fat
  • Spiced Scramble: 5 Large Grade A White Eggs scrambled in Great Value 0-Cal Cooking Spray. Cooking Instructions: Heat non-stick skillet over medium heat, spray liberally. Whisk eggs and pour in. Gently fold with spatula over medium heat. During the last 60 seconds, fold in 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, and chopped scallions until fluffy and golden! (350 kcal, 30g p, 25g f)
  • Post-Workout Carbs: 1 cup cooked Long Grain White Rice. Cooking Instructions: Rinse raw rice until water runs clear. Combine 1 cup rice with 2 cups water in saucepan, bring to boil, cover, and simmer low for 18 minutes. Fluff with fork and season with garlic powder! (200 kcal, 4g p, 44g c)
  • Antioxidant Boost: 1 cup hot brewed 100% Green Tea steeped for 3-5 minutes (0 kcal, post-workout recovery aid)
2:00 PM – 2:30 PM Lunch: Wok-Seared General Tso's Chicken Breast & Broccoli + Tea
440 kcal | 46g Protein | 53g Carbs | 2g Fat
  • General Tso's Chicken: ~5.7 oz Fresh Chicken Breast cubes seared in wok/skillet. Cooking Instructions: Cut chicken into bite-sized 1-inch cubes, toss with 1 tsp cornstarch & garlic powder. Spray wok with 0-cal canola spray over medium-high heat. Sear chicken cubes 6-8 mins until golden brown (165°F internal). Whisk low-cal General Tso glaze (1.5 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sriracha/chili garlic sauce, 1 zero-cal sweetener packet, 1/4 tsp ginger, 1/4 tsp garlic powder, 1 tbsp water). Pour glaze over chicken and toss 1 min until caramelized and sticky! (210 kcal, 40g p, 2g f)
  • Green Micronutrients: 1 cup steamed Great Value Frozen Broccoli florets microwaved with 2 tbsp water for 3-4 mins, drained and seasoned with garlic powder (30 kcal, 2g p, 6g c)
  • Clean Energy: 1 cup cooked Long Grain White Rice reheated warm with a splash of water (200 kcal, 4g p, 44g c)
  • Thermogenic Aid: 1 cup hot brewed 100% Green Tea (0 kcal, fat oxidation booster)
5:30 PM Afternoon Snack: Chinese Casein Greek Yogurt Bowl
120 kcal | 21g Protein | 8g Carbs | 0g Fat
  • Anabolic Casein Bowl: 1 cup (8 oz) Plain Nonfat Greek Yogurt spooned into bowl. Stir in 1 zero-calorie sweetener packet and a pinch of cinnamon or vanilla until creamy. Slow-release casein protein keeps muscles fueled and satiated between meals! (120 kcal, 21g p, 8g c)
7:30 PM – 8:00 PM Dinner: Wok-Seared General Tso's Chicken Breast & Broccoli + Tea
440 kcal | 46g Protein | 53g Carbs | 2g Fat
  • General Tso's Chicken: ~5.7 oz Fresh Chicken Breast cubes wok-seared with 0-cal spray over medium-high heat and tossed with homemade low-cal General Tso glaze (soy sauce, sriracha, sweetener, ginger, garlic powder, water) until caramelized and glossy! (210 kcal, 40g p, 2g f)
  • Green Micronutrients: 1 cup steamed Great Value Frozen Broccoli florets (crisp-tender, seasoned with garlic powder) (30 kcal, 2g p, 6g c)
  • Clean Carbs: 1 cup cooked Long Grain White Rice reheated warm and fluffy (200 kcal, 4g p, 44g c)
  • Recovery Tea: 1 cup hot brewed 100% Green Tea (0 kcal, overnight metabolic aid)
10:30 PM Nighttime Recovery: Casein Greek Yogurt Fluff & Hot Green Tea
90 kcal | 16g Protein | 6g Carbs | 0g Fat
  • Overnight Recovery: 3/4 cup (6 oz) Plain Nonfat Greek Yogurt whisked with 1 zero-calorie sweetener packet until airy and fluffy. Provides slow-release casein amino acids to rebuild muscle fiber while you sleep. (90 kcal, 16g p, 6g c)
  • Metabolic Soother: 1 cup warm soothing 100% Green Tea (0 kcal, overnight deep sleep recovery)
