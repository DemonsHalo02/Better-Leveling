🔥 Daily Meal Blueprint & Cooking Instructions

☕ Pre-Workout Fuel: Death Wish Iced Black Coffee & Banana
8:00 AM (Pre-Workout)
Calories: 110 kcal
Protein: 1g
Carbs: 27g
Fat: 0g
  • 1 Pre-Workout Death Wish Iced Black Coffee: Brew 2 tbsp Death Wish Espresso Roast ground coffee with hot water, pour over ice (optional: add 1 zero-cal sweetener packet or keep pure black). Extreme clean caffeine and focus!
  • 1 Medium Fresh Banana: Peel and eat immediately before heading to Planet Fitness Lewiston for rapid-absorbing pre-workout carbohydrates & potassium!
👨‍🍳 Quick Prep Instructions: Brew 2 tbsp of Death Wish Espresso Roast with 8 oz of hot water directly into a cup with ice, or batch cold-brew a pitcher overnight in your fridge. Eat your banana alongside your coffee exactly at 8:00 AM before walking out the door to Planet Fitness Lewiston!
🍳 Post-Workout Breakfast: Korean Scallion & Egg White Scramble + Kimchi & Rice + Tea
10:30 AM – 11:00 AM (Post-Workout)
Calories: 550 kcal
Protein: 36g
Carbs: 48g
Fat: 15g
  • 3 Large Grade A White Eggs + 1/2 Cup (4 oz) Liquid Egg Whites: Scrambled fluffy with chopped fresh green onions and soy sauce.
  • 1 Cup Cooked Long Grain White Rice: Fluffy, clean, fast-digesting carbohydrates to replenish muscle glycogen post-workout.
  • 2 Tbsp Nasoya Authentic Korean Spicy Kimchi: Fermented napa cabbage for digestive health, gut probiotics, and tangy spice.
  • 1 Cup Hot Brewed Great Value 100% Green Tea: Steeped fresh for antioxidant recovery & metabolism boost.
👨‍🍳 Cooking Instructions: Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and spray liberally with 0-calorie canola oil spray. Whisk 3 whole eggs plus 1/2 cup liquid egg whites in a bowl. Pour into skillet and gently fold with a spatula. During the last 60 seconds of cooking, fold in 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, and fresh chopped green onions until golden and fluffy. Serve over 1 cup of warm white rice with a 2 tbsp side of spicy kimchi and a hot cup of green tea!
🍱 Lunch: Wok-Seared Korean Gochujang Glazed Chicken Breast & Steamed Broccoli + Tea
2:00 PM – 2:30 PM
Calories: 440 kcal
Protein: 46g
Carbs: 53g
Fat: 2g
  • ~5.7 oz Fresh Chicken Breast Cubes (Batch Cooked): Wok-seared and tossed in sticky, caramelized Korean Gochujang / Bibigo Glaze.
  • 1 Cup Steamed Frozen Broccoli Florets: Crisp-tender micronutrients and dietary fiber seasoned with garlic powder.
  • 1 Cup Cooked Long Grain White Rice: Reheated warm and fluffy from Monday's batch prep.
  • 1 Cup Hot Brewed 100% Green Tea: Thermogenic fat oxidation booster.
👨‍🍳 Monday Batch Prep & Reheating Instructions: Cut fresh chicken breasts into bite-sized 1-inch cubes. Toss lightly with 1 tsp cornstarch and garlic powder. Spray non-stick wok or large skillet with 0-cal spray over medium-high heat. Sear chicken cubes for 6-8 minutes until golden and fully cooked (165°F internal). Whisk low-cal Korean Glaze (1.5 tbsp Bibigo Korean BBQ sauce / soy sauce, 1 tsp sriracha, 1 zero-cal sweetener packet, 1/4 tsp ginger, 1/4 tsp garlic powder, and 1 tbsp water). Pour glaze into wok over chicken and toss vigorously for 1 minute until sticky, glossy, and caramelized! Divide into meal prep containers with rice and broccoli. Reheat for 90 seconds and enjoy with hot green tea.

👨‍🍳 Prep Instructions: Spoon 1 full cup (8 oz) of cold plain Greek yogurt into a bowl. Add 1 packet of zero-calorie sweetener and a pinch of ground cinnamon or vanilla extract. Stir vigorously until creamy and dessert-like!
🥢 Dinner: Wok-Seared Korean Gochujang Glazed Chicken Breast & Steamed Broccoli + Tea
7:30 PM – 8:00 PM

  • ~5.7 oz Fresh Chicken Breast Cubes (Batch Cooked): Wok-seared in low-cal Korean Gochujang / Bibigo Glaze.

10:30 PM
